12 September 2026. Policy MCL-DEV-2026-09-08. Read-only diagnosis of the integrated readability correction; no runtime, source, asset, timing or selection edits. [TP checkpoint](THIRD_PERSON_CHECKPOINT.md) owns current status. Daniel rejects TP presentation while reporting that FP animation and the swing feel good. Preserve that FP feel, the authorized 500 ms release, input behavior and canonical damage. The product test is whether the opponent can read windup onset, swing position, movement and look direction.

**The strongest demonstrated defect is a recovery/idle contract mismatch, amplified by unlimited cosmetic arm extension.** Contact correctness was achieved by attaching TP hands to the canonical weapon throughout recovery while retaining a different TP body performance. This does not establish a general rig failure, and does not justify redesigning the accepted FP swing.

## Ranked causes and evidence

1. **Runtime recovery overrides a different authored recovery: demonstrated.** [BindContactPose](../Source/MeleeCombatLab/Visual/TPCombatPresentation.cpp) selects canonical weapon geometry in every native phase except windup. `FitContactArms` retains the authored shoulder/body and solves the wrists to that weapon; [solveArm](../Source/MeleeCombatLab/Visual/MeleePresentationPose.h) deliberately stretches unreachable targets without an artistic limit. In the zero-pitch, stationary first attack, windup after its entry bridge and release closely preserve native geometry; release fit scale is 1.0 and maximum pose displacement is 0.000140807 cm. Recovery exceeds fit scale 1.16 after only 0.100 s and reaches 1.923488587 at recovery +0.566667 s, source age 1.600 s, capture frame 120. Pose displacement there is 78.82 cm. Overall active maximum is 1.923700366 at frame 270, also recovery +0.566667 s. Idle independently begins at scale 1.342728092 with 49.42 cm pose displacement. Thus the major extension is not necessary for the neutral damaging arc.

2. **The sword stop and apparent hand chase have a concrete mechanism: demonstrated geometry, inferred perception.** In the first attack, canonical and visible sword endpoints stop between frames 93 and 94 and remain unchanged through frame 117: 24 stationary sample intervals, 0.400 s, recovery +0.133333 through +0.516667 s. Motion resumes at frame 118. Meanwhile native TP recovery continues: at source age 1.233333 s its hilt/tip move approximately 303/1,252 cm/s over the preceding sampled interval, while the rendered endpoints are stationary. At recovery +0.466667 s the rendered versus native weapon endpoint error reaches 208.10 cm. The solver has no temporal lag: it assigns wrist targets exactly each frame. “Hands chasing” is consistent with the moving body/arm performance being reshaped around a different stationary weapon, not evidence of a delayed IK filter. The FP weapon uses the same canonical trajectory; Daniel accepts its feel. Preserve that result and solve TP coordination around an explicit presentation contract.

3. **Missing leg articulation is present in the source and selected runtime path: demonstrated.** Across all 147 exported source samples, left thigh/knee and both feet remain fixed relative to root within 0.000015 cm; their deformation rotations match root within numerical tolerance. The feet do move in world/source space through root yaw, but there is no independent leg action or weight transfer in these samples. [KnightPresentation](../Source/MeleeCombatLab/Visual/KnightPresentation.cpp) computes a generic locomotion pose, but selected native TP replaces the whole pose during native action/idle. The generic gait is therefore not composed into those selected TP legs. A new rig is not yet justified: this is missing authored lower-body action plus a composition boundary.

4. **Look-down presentation needs a separate controlled check: mechanism established, severity unmeasured.** `BindContactPose` applies gameplay hip-pivot lean to the complete spine subtree, including the head. [BodyFrame](../Source/MeleeCombatLab/Combat/CombatBodyPose.h) and `Combatant::bodyFrame` derive that lean from view pitch and the configured torso scale; selected TP has no independent head/gaze compensation here. This is an explicit presentation transform about the hip, not evidence of broken bone weights. The source receipt describes upright yaw without animated spine pitch. Existing replay pitch spans only 0 to 0.98 degrees; it cannot reproduce Daniel's large downward-look complaint or distinguish an undesirable lean policy from source head posture at that input. At zero pitch the added lean is identity, so it cannot explain the large neutral recovery extension. Do not suppress visible look intent merely to make the body upright.

5. **Phase retiming changes rhythm but does not explain this recovery mismatch.** [EXSourceClock](../Source/MeleeCombatLab/Combat/Attacks/EXSourceClock.h), shared by [TP sampling](../Source/MeleeCombatLab/Visual/TPSourceBinding.h), FP and collision, maps the 0.300 s source release to the authorized 0.500 s runtime release; recovery resumes at source speed with continuous positions. Derivative changes at phase boundaries can affect rhythm. They are distinct from the stationary canonical recovery versus moving TP source, and changing approved timing is not the first response.

Daniel additionally reports a sword held too far forward at idle, an off-balance torso, and forward bend combined with fully extended arms. The sampled canonical idle hilt is 62.0 cm forward and 10.04 cm lateral to actor position at yaw zero; binding forces the separate native TP ready to that position, already producing 1.343 fit scale. This demonstrates a ready-target compatibility problem. It does not prove a skinning defect. Diagnosing skin weights requires an unobscured shoulder/elbow deformation under known, reasonable joint transforms; the existing tracer-obscured screenshot cannot establish that. Re-rigging before separating target placement, added lean and native pose would confound three different causes.

Acceptance evidence: full-speed human A/B for load onset, curved launch, connected arms, coordinated braking/carry and readable gaze; source-versus-runtime upper/lower link lengths and shoulder displacement through recovery/idle; continuous endpoint/velocity behavior at the changed bridges; unchanged damaging release alignment and existing FP feel. Existing native source allows about 16% authored extension, whereas telemetry reports *additional fitting relative to sampled native lengths*; these are different measures, not interchangeable percentages. Treat new extension above the retained source envelope as a defect to explain, not a contact-success metric. Do not claim Mordhau quality from sampled poses, zero endpoint error or a critic score.

If an acceptable TP body/weapon path cannot coexist with the preserved FP/canonical geometry, explicitly decide which nondamaging visual differences are permitted or which TP body placement/choreography changes are acceptable. Any change to gameplay contact, timing or FP feel requires that explicit design decision; never hide the disagreement with larger arm stretch.
