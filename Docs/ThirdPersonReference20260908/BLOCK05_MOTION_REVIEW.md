# Block05 motion review

Reviewed the frozen dense source `87aed0c7` keys in `ArtSource/CharacterReset/TP_v001/Preview/FrontThreeQuarter` (57, 60, 63, 68, 81, 88, 94, 100), compared against the Block04 front-three-quarter keys, and inspected native defender/external frames 52, 63, and 81. This is a still-frame readability review; normal-speed human acceptance was not performed.

The intended right-horizontal action is readable: the chamber is pronounced, both hands stay visibly engaged, the blade travels through a distinct horizontal extension, and the apex carries a bent/downward elbow silhouette. Stylized deformation is acceptable for this review.

Two remaining issues are worth addressing if another pass is available:

1. **Carry/recovery arm silhouette remains compressed.** In dense frames 68 and especially 81, the forearms and hands fold across the abdomen while the blade turns vertical, so the body reads briefly as a guarded pose instead of the end of a large rightward swing. Keep the recovery timing, but preserve a small outward elbow offset and a visible forearm-to-hilt gap through the carry. This is a silhouette/readability adjustment, not a natural-reach or physics constraint.

2. **Defender close view can hide the contact beat.** Native defender frame 52 has the incoming blade, defender blade, and first-person hands crossing in the lower-center/right area; frame 63 is clearer once the attacker has extended. Ensure the impact/deflection pose has a short readable separation between the two blade lines (or a slightly higher defender guard) so the contact reads as a beat before the attacker passes through. Avoid changing the established horizontal path.

No additional blocking issue was found in the supplied keys. The dense source is suitable as the preview candidate pending a normal-speed gameplay check.
