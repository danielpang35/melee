## Required critic loop

1. **Reference contract before authoring.** Supply the critic one action-specific record: source ID/time, actor, POV, weapon, neutral/riposte, origin, preceding parry side, manipulation, visible beats, occlusions and observation mode. Use corrected N-RH as the neutral TP comparator. Keep supplementary A/B, FP, Ren and Kronk examples in their own roles; never splice another actor's answering hands into the opener's return.
2. **Review a batch together.** Present H unchanged as control plus 3–4 complete new performances at identical source speed, fixed camera and comparable framing. Every candidate includes ready departure, load, delivery, carry and return. Record actual source/control/preview identity. Rank intent, load/delivery contrast, hilt/blade travel, coordination and recovery; do not rank by elbow error or tool success.
3. **Make the critique executable.** The reused independent critic returns a provisional winner or rejects the batch; cites candidate frames/source times; separates observation from interpretation; lists at most three prioritized corrections; states one dominant next hypothesis, expected visible result and smallest next preview. Include regression risks to the liked body drive, restrained wrists and carry. Record why a score changed. C2/C3/C7/C9 may be scoped judgments; temporal dimensions stay NE when only sheets were inspected.
4. **Refine one winner, then compare again.** Review each meaningful revision against its immediate parent, H and the same reference excerpt. Require convincing whole-action gain. After two weak refinement passes, restart the choreography batch or conduct one bounded authoring-control experiment. Do not relabel elbow variants as new artistic hypotheses. Reuse this critic through refinement; no duplicate reviews or child agents.
5. **Keep approval states separate.** Agent selection means promising source draft. Continuous 1x review supports rhythm/power judgments only if actually observed. Selected native defender gameplay supports phase/reach/contact and outcome judgments. Human acceptance remains Daniel's decision. The rubric's 8/10 gameplay-distance target cannot be earned by a good isolated silhouette, reference resemblance, numeric path equality or a successful import. No aggregate parity claim while critical motion/gameplay evidence is missing.

## First complete-motion hypotheses

These are proposed experiments, not facts recovered from Mordhau. Retain shared timing and canonical damaging motion unless a separate integration decision explicitly changes that contract. Coauthor body, both arms, grips and sword throughout each performance.

| Candidate | Main artistic hypothesis | Whole-action result to compare |
|---|---|---|
| A: Side-loaded cut | Move non-damaging preparation toward an origin-side shoulder load with a moving, eased apex; stage shoulders below the crossing hand mass, then absorb into low carry. | Clearer right-origin intent and head separation without a jump into the fixed release path or loss of delivery force. |
| B: Sequenced body drive | Establish load in pelvis/chest first, let shoulders and arms open later through delivery, then let chest/hips finish and settle with the carry. | Weight reads as a connected chain rather than arms arranged around a blade; avoid excessive lean, renewed wrist cant or decorative counter-motion. |
| C: Rounded approach and absorbed exit | Author a curved non-damaging hilt approach into release, progressively open the elbows, and carry torso effort through the weapon's low exit before returning. | Remove abrupt directional changes and broad held crossing shapes while preserving a distinct attack, follow-through and ready return. |

All three preserve useful H/F qualities as artistic goals, not immutable non-release keys. If the grip path itself forces the unwanted reach or face crossing, report the incompatible constraint with the specific interval. A broader isolated path study may then be useful, but it cannot silently alter accepted FP or authoritative contact. Avoid claiming the body-only batch can solve an impossible hand-position constraint.
