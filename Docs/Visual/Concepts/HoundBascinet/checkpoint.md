COMPOSITION: One single high-resolution landscape image, two equally large complete figures side by side: FRONT on the left, BACK on the right. Crown to sole fully visible, generous margins; same adult wearer, same armor set, identical height and stance, aligned feet and head levels. True straight-on front and straight-on rear views with minimal perspective, neutral upright stance, feet slightly apart, arms relaxed a little away from torso to expose construction, empty hands naturally relaxed. No extra inset portraits. Focus on useful complete armor design rather than action. Rear view must show rear skull, rear aventail, coherent shoulder attachment, back torso/cloth, waist, rear legs and heel construction; no front visor appearing on rear head. Resolve unseen lower body and rear as restrained, plausible extensions of the supplied Hound study; consistently map plate silhouettes, straps, blue cloth, gauntlets and articulation across both views.

ART DIRECTION: Match the refined stylized game concept rendering in the references: naturally proportioned medieval armor; continuous convex metal shells with deliberate ridges, thin rims and small bright construction-edge glints; broad irregular angular steel value/reflection regions that describe smooth curved volume. Rich cool-steel / warm-highlight contrast, dark matte mail and quilted padding, quiet heraldic-blue textile areas, sparse brown leather and small brass fasteners. Major silhouette first, material/overlap groups second, rivets and selective wear last. Visor and silhouette should remain immediately legible at reduced size. Maintain generous calm surfaces against selective authored detail. No all-over camouflage micro-patches, no uniform triangulated geometry, no thick white outlines, no toy anatomy, no gigantic shoulders, no fantasy spikes or ornament, no photoreal generic PBR, no gritty grime wash. Natural functional limbs and five-finger gauntlets, connected joints and feet firmly grounded. Back view shows a sensible matching rear textile panel, back armor edges and fastening construction, not a cape.

PRESENTATION: Refined warm pale limestone/parchment-toned study background with an extremely subdued suggestion of the sunlit medieval courtyard, minimal texture and no scenery competing with the figures. Consistent warm daylight and cool fill, controlled highlights, soft contact shadows. Small elegant understated serif title "HOUND BASCINET" at top, small labels "FRONT" and "BACK" beneath their corresponding figures. No other text, watermark or UI.
